Bit Defender: A Farming Simulator
Made by Pinzariu Albert-Andrei

The Game's main Idea and objective: To get out of the life you settled in and to aim for something better. This is represented in the game by reaching the goal of having 10000 Bytes(money)
Operating principles and functionalities: The game loop if the following: you choose a command -> you advance the time -> you see what happens. You have at your disposal multiple tools that help you achieve your goal.
                                          There are 9 main commands: planting(letting you plant from 4 different plants in exchange for money), harvesting(letting you sell your crops when they are done growing),
                                           buying(where you can buy enhancers for your crops and new abilities) and next(whice reprezents you letting the time pass)
                                           But, there are also other miscellaneous command that either represent upgraded tools of the previously mentioned commands or they are the usual menu buttons.

I took into account two Sustainable Development Goals while making this game. The first one being Health and well-being, as the protagonist of our story is in the persue of self betterment and self reflection.
The second Goal is the Sustainable Industry, by planting, selling and rebuying crops, the character actively supports the industry.
